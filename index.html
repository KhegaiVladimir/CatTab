Cattab — Privacy Policy

Cattab does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

All data (pet stats, site blocklist, settings) is stored locally in your browser using chrome.storage.local and never leaves your device.

Cattab does not make any network requests. It has no servers, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party integrations.

The extension requires access to all pages solely to display the animated pet on top of web content using a Shadow DOM overlay. Page content is never read, stored, or transmitted.
